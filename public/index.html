A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Frequently asked
questions.

What is SAVRO?

SAVRO is a web-based bookkeeping and business management workspace for organizing sales, expenses, invoices, inventory and business reports.

Who can use SAVRO?

SAVRO is designed for small and growing businesses that want a clearer way to organize everyday business activity.

Can I track both income and expenses?

Yes. SAVRO lets you record income and expenses and review them through your business dashboard and reports.

Does SAVRO include inventory and invoices?

Yes. The workspace includes invoice management, an item catalogue and inventory tracking.

How do I get started?

Choose Get started to open the SAVRO account page, create your workspace and select your trial or plan.
